## Thread under the pinned post

2/
The curve comes in six shapes, all real Meteora curves: two market caps, an amount raised, two segments, a middle price, liquidity weights, or your own prices. It graduates to a Meteora pool at the market cap or raise you set, against SOL or USDC.

3/
Fees on the curve: your base fee, an anti-sniper fee that starts at 50% and falls to your fee over the first minute (you choose how high and how long), and an optional volatility fee that rises while the price swings.

4/
After graduation the pool charges a Meteora tier or your own fee from 0.1% to 10%. It can also step down as the market cap grows, to a floor you set.

5/
The fees go to one wallet, or to 2 to 5 wallets in fixed shares through Meteora's fee-sharing vault. Each wallet claims its own share.

6/
Also in the form: liquidity locked for good or not, token vesting through Jupiter's locker, a Token-2022 transfer tax up to 10%, and a start time up to a year ahead.

7/
Every token page lists what it launched with: mint and freeze authority, locked metadata, any tax, and how much liquidity is locked. Holders can check before they buy.

8/
T-minus builds the transactions and your wallet signs them. It never holds your keys, your fees or your liquidity.

9/
What T-minus takes: 10% of the curve's fees after Meteora's cut, and 10% of the pool's liquidity after graduation, always locked. A plain pool costs 0.1 SOL once.

How claiming, graduation and fees work: tminus.si/docs

Already have a token? T-minus makes it a Meteora pool. Deposit both sides to set the starting price, or put in tokens only from a price you set. Lock the liquidity for good if you want to. It costs 0.1 SOL once.
