## Platform

web

## Users

Two groups, served equally, with speed first (owner, 2026-10-16):

- Projects and teams launching a real token on Meteora who want control over it: fees routed to a treasury, splits across wallets, vesting and locked liquidity. The first real launch is a casino token team with a dev wallet and a treasury wallet.
- Quick creators who want a token live fast and may never open the advanced settings.

Both sign in with a Solana wallet and sign every transaction in it. Many are beginners with Meteora's own terms.

## Product Purpose

T-minus is the all-in-one launchpad for Meteora. One site creates the token, shapes the bonding curve or plain pool, decides where the fees go, locks or vests liquidity, and manages it all after launch, with no code. Success: a creator gets from an idea to a live token with exactly the settings they meant, and can find and claim their fees afterwards.

## Positioning

Full autonomy and flexibility over every setting Meteora's programs allow (DBC curves, DAMM v2 pools, fee routing, locks), where other Meteora launchers (Jupiter Studio, Bags, Believe, Star.fun) are fixed products and Meteora's Invent CLI needs a developer. T-minus never holds keys, fees or liquidity: Meteora's programs route the fees to the wallets the creator chose.

## Operating Context

- A launch is a wizard: the token, how it trades (bonding curve or plain pool), fees, where the fees go, a review read back from the built transactions, then signing (one or two transactions).
- Presets start a launch fast; every setting stays reachable behind them.
- After launch: a Manage page for everything the wallet controls (claim fees, hand over the creator role, lock, split, merge, send positions), a public token page per launch, and an Explore list.
- Buying and selling happen on each token page, quoted and routed by Jupiter with T-minus's referral fee on top (owner, 2026-10-17). It is a buy/sell panel, not a trading terminal: no charts, order types or portfolio.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Six curve shapes, a 0.25 to 99% curve fee with a falling anti-sniper fee (on by default), up to five wallets in a fee split, SOL, USDC or any SPL token as the quote token, Token-2022 options, liquidity locked forever or vested.
- Every transaction is simulated before signing and has to match the review. The review is built from the transactions, not the form.
- Settings that can never change after launch are marked with a lock and confirmed.
- Not in the first version: DLMM, Alpha Vault, Presale Vault, farming, a full trading screen (charts, limit orders), chains other than Solana.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is T-minus, on tminus.si (owner, 2026-10-17; it was Meteor, "the aio meteor launchpad"). User-facing copy says T-minus, never "the studio".
- Headlines lead with the full control the product gives, never with one use case or preset (the owner rejected a hero built around the casino fee-to-treasury preset).
- Flat (owner, 2026-10-17): no shadows, glass or glow anywhere; hairlines and space separate surfaces; 6–8px corners, no pills.
- Minimal black and white (owner, 2026-10-16, replacing the earlier ice-blue look the owner found cheap): near-white grounds, a neutral grey scale, near-black ink, Satoshi type, the wordmark in Satoshi Bold lowercase. No accent colour: the large black buttons carry the T-minus mark in white (owner's choice over a blue ring).
- The home page keeps the 3D meteorite with its bouncing shards, rendered as smoky glass in greys (owner's choice).
- Settings that can never change are marked with a lock in ink and confirmed on the review.
- Plain words first, with Meteora's own term in small print beside them.
- Marketing and UI copy goes through the better-writing skill.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real launches exist only on the local mainnet fork (e.g. "Ice Chip"). There are no customers, testimonials or volume figures yet; none may be invented.
- Presets come from the API (/api/presets).

## Product Principles

1. Every setting Meteora allows is reachable; presets are a shortcut, never a ceiling.
2. Nothing irreversible happens without being named in plain words and confirmed.
3. What you review is what you sign.
4. Fast for the quick creator, deep for the project, in the same flow.
5. T-minus never holds anyone's keys, fees or liquidity.
